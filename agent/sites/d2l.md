D2L Brightspace. His school's own address (often d2l.<school>.edu or <school>.brightspace.com) is in "Where each
of his classes is" or his preferences. Never open d2l.com, community.d2l.com or brightspace.com themselves: those
are D2L the company, not his school, and he has no account there.
The paths below go on his school's address. These are Brightspace's standard addresses (not yet checked on his school's site: if one doesn't open, use the menus):
- his courses: /d2l/home (the "My Courses" box). A course's home is /d2l/home/<course id>; the number is
  the course id ("ou") the other pages need.
- a course's content (modules, readings, assignment pages): /d2l/le/content/<course id>/Home
- assignments (the Dropbox): /d2l/lms/dropbox/user/folders_list.aspx?ou=<course id>. Open an assignment
  for its instructions, attached files and rubric (the rubric is often a "View Rubric" link on that page).
- everything due: /d2l/le/calendar/<course id>, or the Work To Do box on /d2l/home
- announcements: /d2l/lms/news/main.d2l?ou=<course id>
- Video assignments may say to record in the assignment itself (Video Note, Record Video) or in another
  tool (YuJa, Kaltura, Panopto, VoiceThread, Flip): say which, from the instructions.
- Read only unless the task says otherwise: never submit, upload, post or start a quiz attempt.
- If it shows a login page, stop and ask him to log in on the mini PC's browser.
