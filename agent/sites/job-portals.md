Where applications actually get sent. "Apply" on a posting opens the form; the button that sends it
is always some "Submit", and that one waits for his yes.
- **LinkedIn:** "Easy Apply" is a popup of a few short pages (contact, resume, questions, review)
  with Next; the last button is "Submit application". Plain "Apply" leaves for the employer's site.
  Needs him logged in. The resume page lists resumes already on his account: pick his, or "Upload
  resume" for the file. Untick "Follow <company>" if it's ticked.
- **Indeed:** "Apply now" (Indeed Apply) is a multi-page form ending on a Review page with "Submit
  your application"; "Apply on company site" leaves Indeed. Logged in, it remembers his details.
- **Workday** (*.myworkdayjobs.com): "Apply" → "Autofill with Resume" / "Apply Manually" / "Use My
  Last Application". It needs an account on that employer's Workday (email + password): making one
  is his. Pages: My Information, My Experience (the resume upload is here), Application Questions,
  Voluntary Disclosures, Self Identify, Review, then "Submit". Fields that suggest as you type
  (school, skills, job title) only count when the suggestion is clicked.
- **Greenhouse** (boards.greenhouse.io, job-boards.greenhouse.io, or embedded on a careers page): one
  long page. "Attach" next to Resume/CV opens the file box ("Enter manually" is the other way), the
  demographic questions are at the bottom, "Submit application" last. Usually no login.
- **Lever** (jobs.lever.co): one page, "Attach resume/CV" (it may autofill name and email from it),
  "Submit application". No login.
- **Handshake:** needs his school login. "Apply" is a popup that picks from documents already on his
  Handshake account; a missing one must be uploaded on his Documents page first. "Submit application".
- **iCIMS:** wants an account, or a one-time code by email (stop and ask for the code).
  **SmartRecruiters / Ashby:** one page, resume box near the top, "Submit" / "Submit application".
- **Glassdoor / ZipRecruiter / WayUp:** mostly hand off to one of the above or the employer's site.
- All of them push "Sign in with Google / LinkedIn", job alerts and newsletters: decline. An
  application fee is never paid.
