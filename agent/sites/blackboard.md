Blackboard Ultra. His school's own address (<school>.blackboard.com) is in his preferences.
- **Go by address, not the menu**: the left menu button didn't respond to clicks. Open these directly on his school's address:
  - his courses: /ultra/course
  - everything due, across courses: /ultra/calendar (the "Due Dates" view)
  - recent activity and announcements: /ultra/stream
  - grades: /ultra/grades
- A course opens at /ultra/courses/<course id>/outline: find his course on the courses page, then open it.
  Its other pages (Discussions, Gradebook…) have no address of their own (/discussions says "Check the
  address"): open the outline and use the course's own menu.
- A Learning Module opens and closes with one click on its title. Click it once, then use browser_find with
  words from the item you want; clicking the title again closes it.
- A module's items are links named by kind: "Quiz, Ch 8 Quiz", "Discussion, Chapter 8 Discussion - …". The
  checkbox above each says whether he has done it ("Status for Ch 8 Quiz: Completed").
- A discussion opens from its link in the module, in the same tab. Its questions may be a picture you can't
  read: say so instead of guessing them.
- McGraw Hill Connect items (quizzes, homework) open in a new tab (connect…mheducation.com).
- Read only unless the task says otherwise: never submit, post or start a test or quiz attempt.
- If it shows a login page, stop and ask him to log in on the mini PC's browser.
