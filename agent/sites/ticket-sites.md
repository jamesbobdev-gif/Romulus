- The usual order: pick the date, then a time slot or section, then how many tickets, then checkout.
- Popular nights sell out: if his date or time is gone, list what's left nearby instead of choosing.
- Watch for extra fees added at checkout; report the total the page shows, fees included. Resale
  sites (SeatGeek, StubHub, Vivid Seats) list prices before fees: open the listing for the real total.
- Ticketmaster, AXS and the resale sites hold picked seats for a few minutes (a timer on the page):
  go straight to the review page and ask for his yes; don't keep browsing with seats on hold.
- Checkout asks for his name, email and a card: fill what his Form answers give, never the card.
  With a card already saved on the site, the final "Place order" / "Buy now" / "Pay" waits for his yes
  (the tickets skill says how); without one, stop and let him enter it.
- "Ticket insurance", "upgrade to VIP", parking and merch are add-ons: decline them.
