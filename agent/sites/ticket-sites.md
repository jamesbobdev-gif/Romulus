- The usual order: pick the date, then a time slot, then how many tickets, then checkout.
- Popular nights sell out: if his date or time is gone, list what's left nearby instead of choosing.
- Watch for extra fees added at checkout; report the total the page shows, fees included.
- Checkout asks for his name, email and a card: fill what his Form answers give, never the card, and
  stop before the final "Pay"/"Place order".
