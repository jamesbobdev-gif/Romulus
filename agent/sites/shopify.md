- Many small stores run on Shopify: the checkout page has a purple Shop Pay button, and the address is
  the store's own site (…/checkouts/…) or shop.app.
- Shop Pay keeps his saved card and address for every Shopify store. If it asks for a code it texted
  him, that step is his (the shopping skill says how to hand it over).
- The product page's "Buy with Shop Pay" button skips the cart; use Add to cart, then Check out, so he
  sees the full review first.
- Shipping costs and times differ a lot between small stores: put both in the note.
