- Google Flights (google.com/travel/flights) and Kayak are for comparing; book on the airline's own
  site, where his account and saved card are. Google Flights' "Book" buttons just hand off to them.
- Southwest isn't on Google Flights or Kayak: check southwest.com separately for domestic trips.
- Fare tiers: Basic economy is cheapest but often can't choose seats, change, or bring a full
  carry-on; say which tier an option is. The price shown first is usually per person before bags.
- Traveler details (name as on ID, date of birth, gender) are required; a passport only for
  international. Never type a passport number.
- Airlines push trip insurance, seat upgrades, priority boarding and their credit card at every step:
  decline all of them unless he asked.
- The final button is "Purchase", "Pay now", "Book" or "Complete purchase"; it waits for his yes.
