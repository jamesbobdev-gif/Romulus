- Link a product as amazon.com/dp/<the 10-character code>; leave out the tracking parts after it.
- "Sponsored" results are ads: skip them unless they're really the best match.
- Price, rating and "ships by" change often: say what the page showed and when.
- Several sellers can sell the same item: prefer "Ships from and sold by Amazon" or a well-rated seller.
- "Add to Cart" only once he's picked the item; never "Buy Now" (it can order in one tap). "Place your
  order" waits for his yes (the shopping skill says how).
- Checkout pushes extras (Prime trial, protection plans, Amazon card offers, "Deliver faster for $x"):
  decline them. Keep the free shipping option unless he asked for faster.
