Booking site for barbers, salons and similar services.
- The usual order: pick the service(s), then the professional (or "any"), then a day and time.
- Times shown are the business's open slots; if his preferred time isn't there, list the closest 2–3
  instead of picking one for him.
- Confirming needs him **logged in** (phone or email, often with a text code). Don't create an
  account. Stop before the final "Confirm"/"Book" and say what's chosen: service, person, day, time,
  price.
- Some places ask for a card to hold the booking: that's his to enter.
