Internship and entry-level job listings.
- Listing pages are readable, but applying needs him **logged in**. If you hit a login or sign-up
  page, stop and tell him to log in to WayUp in the mini PC's browser.
- "Apply" often sends you to the **employer's own site** (a new address): read that page fresh, the
  employer's form is what matters. That site may have its own note.
- Listings expire: check the posting still shows an Apply button and note the deadline if one is shown.
