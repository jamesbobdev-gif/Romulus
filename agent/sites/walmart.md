- Grocery items say "EBT eligible" on the product page. Checkout lets you split the total between his
  EBT card and another payment.
- Pickup vs delivery and the time slot are chosen at checkout. Slots fill up, so say which one you held.
- Check the cart page for "out of stock" or "unavailable" lines before checkout, since they're easy to miss.
- The EBT PIN is entered on a secure pop-up at checkout. Never type it: that step is his.
- "Sorry, we couldn't process your payment: You have not been charged" at the top of checkout means
  the order failed (often the EBT part: balance too low for the "Benefits" amount, or the PIN pop-up).
  Don't press Place order again; stop and tell him, as the groceries skill says.
- Search results start with "Sponsored" items. Skip them unless they really are the best match.
- Non-grocery items are paid with his saved card (no EBT). Walmart+ trial offers at checkout: decline.
