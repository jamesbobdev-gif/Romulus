Many employers' career sites (e.g. Cummins) run on Oracle Recruiting Cloud: addresses like
fa-….fa.ocs.oraclecloud.com/hcmUI/CandidateExperience/….
- Applying starts with his **email address**, then the site usually emails him a **one-time code**.
  You can't read his email: stop, say a code was sent, and ask him to reply with it (status needs-you).
- The application is several pages (contact details, experience/resume, questions, sometimes an
  e-signature). "Next"/"Continue" are fine; the last "Submit" is his call.
- Resume upload is usually required, often offered first as "Autofill with resume": attach his resume
  from his files for forms (the upload tool), then check every field it filled against his details.
- Questions about work authorization, sponsorship, relocation and demographics (gender, race,
  veteran, disability): use his Form answers. If they don't cover one, leave it empty.
- An e-signature field means typing his full name to sign: that's him agreeing, so leave it for him.
