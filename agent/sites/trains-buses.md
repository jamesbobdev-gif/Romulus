---
name: Trains and buses
sites: amtrak.com, greyhound.com, flixbus.com, megabus.com, wanderu.com, busbud.com
---
- Amtrak: the search form wants From/To station codes (CLE = Cleveland, PHL = Philadelphia 30th St)
  and a date; results list each train with its times, transfers and the cheapest fare. "Value" fares
  are cheapest and mostly non-refundable; "Flex" costs more and can be refunded. Seats on long-distance
  trains (Lake Shore Limited) are coach unless he picks a room.
- Greyhound and FlixBus are one company now and show the same buses; Megabus is separate. Wanderu
  and Busbud compare them all, but book on the bus company's own site.
- Late-night departures from Cleveland are common on these routes: say the times plainly.
- Holiday weeks (Thanksgiving, Christmas) sell out and prices climb: say if the fare is close to the
  last seats.
