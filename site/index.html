Privacy, in short

What you connect stays on the computer you connected it on. No telemetry.

Keys never sync.Tokens and sync keys are encrypted by your operating system and stay on that machine.
Encrypted sync.One button encrypts every list end to end, so the server holds only ciphertext.
Locked means locked.The Locked profile and Finance are never given to a cloud AI.
Checkable builds.Every release has checksums. Details in SECURITY.md.
The AI never sees a card number.Stores use the card they have on file; anywhere else, a one-time Link card for the amount you approved.
Nothing final without you.Buying, paying, sending and booking always wait for your yes.

Questions

The short answers.

What is Romulus?

Romulus is a free, open-source to-do list app with an AI agent built in, for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android. You write a task the way you would tell a person and hand it to the Agent. It searches, fills forms, compares prices and builds the cart, and checks in only when it needs a decision.

Is Romulus free?

Yes. The app is free and MIT licensed, and every installer is built from the public source. Sync between your devices is free up to 5 MB on the Romulus server; a $5 a month plan raises that, or you can point it at your own Supabase project. Most people never need more: a typical list takes about 1% of the free space.

Which AI models does Romulus work with?

Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, OpenRouter, free tiers like Grok and Groq, or your own model through Ollama. You can connect several and it tries them in your order.

Will the Agent buy or send anything without asking?

No. The Agent never buys, sends, submits, books or deletes until you say yes to the exact thing it described, and that rule is enforced in code. The AI never sees a card number: stores use the card they have on file, and anywhere else it gets a one-time Link card for the amount you approved.

Can Romulus order groceries and shop online for me?

Yes. It shops on Amazon, Walmart, Target, Best Buy and Shopify stores, and any other store through a one-time Link card. It builds the cart, tells you the total and delivery time, and waits for your yes before placing the order.

Can I use Romulus with a local AI model and keep private things off the cloud?

Yes. Banking, medical records and passwords go only to your local model through Ollama, and so does the Locked profile that fills in forms. Run the local model on a second computer and it serves every device.

Is Romulus an alternative to Rocket Money?

Its Finance tab covers the same ground without the subscription: every account in one place, bills and subscriptions found for you (Cancel hands one to the Agent), net worth over time and live stock prices. Bank access costs about $15 a year, the data is encrypted on your computer, and no cloud AI ever sees it.

Does Romulus work with Blackboard and D2L?

Yes. The School tab pulls assignments and due dates from Blackboard or D2L, and the Agent can write a brief for things only you can do, like a class video.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not on the App Store yet. The iPhone build runs from Xcode; Windows, macOS, Linux and Android have installers.
