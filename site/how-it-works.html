How it works

Every device reaches out. Nothing reaches in.

Your phone, your computers and an optional model box never connect to each other. Each one talks to a single place, a Supabase project you make, where your lists meet. The cloud AIs you connect only get the questions sent out to them.

What the diagram says

One meeting place

Each list is one row in your own Supabase project, reached only through functions that need that list's key. A change sends a "changed" nudge, so your other devices pull it within a second.

A model box, if you want one

Any spare Linux machine can run Romulus as the worker for your local model. It takes the private and local tasks for all your devices, and its browser is watched live through sync, sealed. There's no VPN, no open port and no address to type.

AIs in your order

Connect the AIs you already pay for or get free. The app tries them in your order and moves on when one is out of usage. Tasks with private words only ever go to your own model.

The models

Leave a model blank and the app picks the best one that AI offers right now ("›" means it tries the next one when a model is busy, over quota or gone).

There is no telemetry. Official builds pass a pairing code through the release's own project, sealed and gone within 10 minutes; nothing else reaches the people who make Romulus. Your keys stay on the computer where you entered them, and Finance never goes to a cloud AI.
