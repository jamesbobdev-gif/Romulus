How it works

Every device reaches out. Nothing reaches in.

Your phone, your computers and an optional model box never connect to each other. Each one talks to one place, the Romulus sync server (or a Supabase project of your own), where your lists meet sealed on the device. The cloud AIs you connect only get the questions sent out to them.

What the diagram says

One meeting place, encrypted

Each list is one row on the sync server, sealed on your device before it leaves, so the server only ever holds ciphertext. Every way in is a function that needs that list's key, and a change sends a "changed" nudge so your other devices pull it within a second. Sync is free up to 50 MB, and you can move to a Supabase project of your own in Settings.

AIs in your order

Connect the AIs you already pay for or get free, even several accounts of one. The app tries them in your order, or on auto picks the one with the most usage left, and moves on when one runs out. Tasks with private words, and anything about your money, only ever go to your own model.

A model box, if you want one

Any spare Linux machine can run Romulus as the worker for your own model. It takes private and local tasks for all your devices, picks up cloud tasks nobody else claimed, and its browser can be watched live through sync, sealed. There's no VPN, no open port and no address to type.

When every computer is off

A phone can hand one Agent task to Romulus cloud, which asks Claude on the server and seals the answer so only the asking device can open it. On a paid plan a repeating task can run there too. Finance, your locked profile and private tasks are never sent.

Notifications that can't be read in transit

When the Agent needs you or finishes, your phone gets a notification you can reply to. Its words are sealed on the computer that sent it, so the server and Google's push service carry only ciphertext; the phone opens and draws it itself.

Yes, enforced in code

Every browser call goes through a guard that refuses buy, pay, send, submit and book until your latest reply is a yes to that exact step, for every AI. Once an order is placed in a run, no other AI in that run can place it again.

The models

Leave a model blank and the app picks the best one that AI offers right now ("›" means it tries the next one when a model is busy, over quota or gone).

There is no telemetry. What reaches the sync server is sealed on your devices, and pairing codes are sealed too and gone within 10 minutes. Your AI keys and calendar sign-ins stay on the computer where you entered them, unless you copy keys to your phone by QR code, and Finance never goes to a cloud AI.
